Construction Committee

The Construction Committee oversees the physical stewardship of our church premises managing renovations, co-ordinating site works, liaising with contractors, and ensuring every structure faithfully serves the congregation.

Our Mandate

01
Renovation & Repairs
Assessing, planning, and overseeing the renovation and repair of all existing church structures to ensure they remain safe, functional, and well-maintained.
02
Site Planning & Coordination
Coordinating site assessments, engaging licensed contractors, reviewing designs, and ensuring all construction work meets appropriate standards.
03
Oversight & Accountability
Monitoring active works, verifying contractor deliverables, managing timelines, and reporting progress to the Church Council and Projects Committee.

How We Work

Assessment
Site visits and structural assessments to identify the scope and urgency of works.
Planning
Preparing specifications, cost estimates, and timelines before seeking council approval.
Contractor Engagement
Inviting and evaluating bids, verifying credentials, and selecting suitable contractors.
Monitoring
Supervising active works on-site and providing regular progress updates to the council.
Handover
Final inspection, sign-off, and documentation before closing each project.
Contractor Engagement Policy
All contractors engaged by the Construction Committee must hold valid registration and carry adequate insurance. Quotations are reviewed collectively by the committee, and all works above a defined threshold require Church Council approval prior to commencement. Members of the congregation with relevant trade qualifications are welcome to register their interest with the committee secretary.
