Projects Committee

The Projects Committee gives shape to the church's future — planning long-term developments, ongoing programs, and ensuring every initiative aligns with the mission of Singgimari Baptist Church.

Projects

Our Mandate

01
Planning Future Developments
Identifying and designing initiatives that expand the church's capacity to serve its congregation and broader community in the years ahead.
02
Ongoing Programs & Initiatives
Coordinating active church programs — ensuring they are properly resourced, on schedule, and delivering meaningful impact.
03
Coordination & Reporting
Maintaining clear communication between departments, the Finance Committee, and the Church Council on all active and proposed projects.

Development Roadmap

2023 – 2024
Foundation & Needs Assessment
The Projects Committee of Singgimari Baptist Church identifies community needs, evaluates church priorities, and establishes strong foundations for spiritual, social, and developmental programs benefiting members and surrounding communities.
2025
Active Program Launch
The committee actively launches programs focused on ensuring effective participation, organized management, and meaningful impact through church-led initiatives.
2026
Community Outreach Planning
The committee plans outreach activities including social service, aid distribution, and fellowship programs to strengthen relationships, spread Christian values, and support the spiritual and practical needs of communities.
2027 – 2028
Long-Range Development
The Projects Committee develops long-term planning for church growth, infrastructure improvement, leadership development, financial sustainability, and future ministry expansion to ensure lasting impact and continued service to God.
