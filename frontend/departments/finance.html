This page offers a transparent overview of how church funds are received, recorded, approved, and reviewed. Rather than publishing private transaction records, we present the lifecycle of financial stewardship — from congregational offerings through to council-reviewed audit entries — so every member may understand how resources serve our shared mission.

External Entity
Process Node
Data Store
Standard Flow
Critical Flow
Audit / Oversight
P1
Collection & Counting
Offerings and tithes from the congregation are received, counted by designated counters, and deposited into the church fund.
P2
Recording & Categorisation
All fund movements — income and ministry expenditure requests — are logged, categorised, and prepared for the approval stage.
P3
Approval & Authorisation
Expenditure requests undergo formal authorisation before any disbursement is made, with notices forwarded to the church council.
P4
Audit & Review
Periodic reviews of the financial ledger ensure accuracy and transparency, with findings returned to recording and reported to the council.
✦

Transparency Note — Detailed transaction records and financial statements are available to church members upon request at the church office. This diagram reflects our stewardship process in accordance with 1 Corinthians 4:2 — "Now it is required that those who have been given a trust must prove faithful."
