Association and Fellowship

Singgimari Baptist Church is part of a wider fellowship within the Garo Baptist Convention.

  • Garo Baptist Convention The central fellowship body of Garo Baptist churches.
    • Krimas Regional fellowships formed under the Convention.
      • Krima-X The Krima to which our church belongs. (Krima No. 10)
        • Singgimari Baptist Church A local church within this Krima fellowship. Establishment year 2022

What We Believe

Our beliefs are rooted in the Bible and Baptist doctrine.

  • We affirm faith in one God, revealed as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • We believe the Bible is the inspired and authoritative Word of God, guiding us in faith, truth, and daily life.
  • We believe redemption comes through God’s grace, received by faith in Christ.
  • We recognize the Church as Christ’s body, called to worship, serve, and witness.
  • We hold to biblical teaching and place Jesus Christ at the center of all we believe and live.

History of Church and the Community

5 minute Read

The church has a recorded history that reflects its establishment, growth, and organizational development over time.

  • Pre-1926 – In the years before 1926, the soil of Singgimari known as Nepal Ading was home to Nepalese families.
    • During this time, no Garo families had yet settled in the area, and the message of the Gospel through our traditions had not yet arrived.
  • 1926 to 1930 – The Lord guided our forefathers from various regions across the Garo Hills to this place.
    • In 1926, Nudan R. Marak and Rangmi A. Sangma (Rutmoni Pa), husband and wife, migrated from Chokpot Bibagre and took shelter for several years at Dengasi Village, staying with Rangmi’s brother-in-law, Rangkil. In 1930, they moved to Singgimari in search of agricultural land for cultivation and permanent settlement.
    • By 1934, others joined them, including Jiran Rema (Ameng Pa), Rengdi Marak, Tina (Bijon Pa) from Samanda, Mohan Ch. Marak, and Ramsi A. Sangma (Repol Pa) from Karukol.
    • Later, Prem Pantho (Lemon Pa) and his wife Gajak Ch. Marak, along with Singdon (Nangjeng Pa), Kanjing (Mehal Pa), and Sese B. Marak from different parts of the Garo Hills, gathered and began living together as one united community.
  • 1934 – Moved by a hunger for fellowship, these forefathers raised the first house of worship in Singgimari.
    • The first generation who laid the foundation of the church in Singgimari provided both spiritual leadership and practical service.
    • The pioneers of this generation were:
      • Dingsan N. Marak (Sunil Pa)
      • Kanjing Sangma (Mehal Pa)
      • Singdon Sangma (Nangjeng Pa)
    • They remained steadfast in prayer for four years, but the congregation was unable to sustain itself, and the doors eventually closed.
  • 1964 – The spirit of revival moved once again. For a second time, a dedicated effort was made to re-establish the church.
    • The church came into being on land given by Sajing Sangma and was opened with prayer in the presence of Bithindro A. Sangma, Pastor, Selsella Baptist Church.
    • The pioneers of the 2nd generation were:
      • Nangjeng Marak (Premosh Pa)
      • Ibang B. Marak (Linder Pa)
      • Kanai M. Marak (Jorina Pa)
      • Samson T. Sangma (Modil Pa)
      • Kanjing Sangma (Mehal Pa)
    • This effort did not continue successfully in the years that followed. As a result, the church again became inactive, marking the second failure to sustain organized church life during that period.
  • 1985 – The third generation, under the leadership of Loru Ch. Marak, renewed outreach and personal engagement with neighboring families, leading to the rebuilding of the church at the former site given by Sajing Sangma and Silme B. Marak.
    • Pioneers of the 3rd generation were:
      • Loru Ch. Marak
      • Samson T. Sangma
      • Wilson R. Marak
      • Ibang B. Marak
      • Helen T. Sangma
      • Ramesh M. Marak
      • Mirendra Ch. Marak
      • Premosh A. Sangma
      • Uselna T. Sangma
      • Lemoni Ch. Marak
      • Bimoni Ch. Marak
      • Lindarson A. Sangma
      • Hebalson T. Sangma
    • These leaders had a shared vision for the future of the church and the community, and through faithful service and unity, they laid the foundation for a permanent church building.
    • On 26/05/1985, our church building was inaugurated by Heavinson A. Sangma under the theme “Torom Nokni Gamchatani,”The Sacred Worth of the House of Prayer drawn from Isaiah 56:7, and by the guidance of the Holy Spirit, our church continues to stand today as generations gather to worship Almighty God.
  • 1998 – The Singgimari community church held a meeting on 17/10/1998 and approved a proposal to become a branch of Selsella Baptist Church (Headquarters) under the leadership of Mr. Milbathson M. Sangma Associate Pastor and Principal Ch. Marak Secretary.
    • As part of the ongoing process, the approved records and draft proposal letters were placed before the 12th Mamong Branch, Roll Soba on 13/12/1998 for consideration and review.
  • 1999 – During the 39th Mondoli Krima Soba, Headquarter Selsella Baptist Church organized Achik Baptist Singgimari Bipek Branch as a branch of Selsella Baptist Church, marking an important step in the church’s growth and fellowship.
    • Pioneers of the Bipek Branch Formation:
      • General Group: Mr. Ibang B. Marak Formerly Pastor, Mr. Milbathson M. Sangma Associate Pastor, Mr. Principal Ch. Marak Secretary, Mr. Wilson R. Marak Accountant, Mrs. Euselna T. Sangma Treasury.
      • Women's Group: Mrs. Bulbulla T. Sangma Chairman, Mrs. Merrytasilla Ch. Marak Secretary, Mrs. Tutha D. Shira Accountant, Mrs. Jorina M. Sangma Treasury, Mrs. Bimoni Ch. Marak Work Secretary.
      • BYF Group Mr. Tarseng A. Sangma Chairman, Mr. Jeremiah A. Sangma V/Chairman, Mrs. Mickrosina A. Sangma Secretary, Mrs. Salchi A. Sangma Joint Secretary, Mr. Operastar A. Sangma Accountant, Mrs. Renish A. Sangma Treasury, Mr. Henith M. Marak Work Secretary.
      • Pillars: Mr. Horendro A. Sangma, Mr, Ramesh M.Marak, Mr. Premitson Marak, Mr. Rajaram Rema, Mr. Junestar A. Sangma, Mrs. Lemony Ch. Marak, Mrs. Premosh A. Sangma, Mrs. Emprojini A. Sangma, Mrs. Tutha D. Shira, Mrs. Jorina M. Marak, Mrs. Neheny A. Sangma, Mrs. Bimoni Ch. Marak.
